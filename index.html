The Importance of Taking Screen Breaks

Spending long periods looking at digital screens can make reading feel uncomfortable. Taking regular breaks allows your eyes to relax and can reduce digital eye strain. A useful habit is to occasionally look away from the screen and focus on something farther away. Adjusting text size, brightness and contrast can also make digital reading more comfortable.

PRACTICAL GUIDANCE

Everyday comfort habits

  • Take regular breaks during long screen sessions.
  • Keep your screen at a comfortable viewing distance.
  • Adjust brightness and contrast according to your environment.
  • Blink regularly and avoid prolonged uninterrupted screen use.
  • Seek professional eye-care advice if symptoms persist or worsen.
